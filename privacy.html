Privacy Notice

How we handle your data on this site.

1. The Data We Collect

This site has no accounts and no forms — we do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details. Our hosting provider (Firebase Hosting) processes standard connection logs (such as IP address and User-Agent string) as technically required to serve and secure the site.

2. Cookies & Preferences

We utilize standard local storage and essential cookies to store your preferences (such as dark mode choice and cookie consent). We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics. No advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking cookies are set.

3. Earlier Waitlist Sign-ups

The waitlist has been retired. If you joined it while it was open, your email address remains stored securely on encrypted database clusters, is not used for any new purpose, and is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

4. Your Rights & Contact

You retain full control over your data. To request deletion of a previously submitted email or to ask anything about this notice, email us at support@useosmos.com.
