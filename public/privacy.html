Privacy Notice

How we handle your data for the Osmos Alpha.

1. Information Collection

We only collect your email address when you sign up for the Osmos Alpha waitlist. No other personal identifiers or tracking metadata are captured.

2. Processing & Security

Your email is transmitted securely via Firebase Cloud Functions and handshaked directly through our dedicated Google Workspace SMTP relays. We encrypt and store subscribers' list data securely.

3. Third-party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or distribute your email to third parties. We use it solely to send waitlist confirmations and official Osmos updates.

4. Opt-out

If you wish to be removed from the waitlist at any time, please contact us at support@useosmos.com and we will purge your email from our directory instantly.
